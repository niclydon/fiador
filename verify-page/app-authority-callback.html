Fiador / Application authority

One step left

Discord sent your browser to this page to finish an application-authority ceremony. Nothing on this page sends anything anywhere — the last step happens back in Discord, with the address of this page.

Finish in Discord

1. Look at your browser's address bar. It holds a long address for this page, ending with parameters that include code= and state=.

2. Copy the entire address, exactly as shown, from the beginning to the end.

3. Return to Discord and run the /fiador authority-complete command: give it the ceremony ID from authority-start and paste the copied address as the callback_url.

The code in the address is single-use, proves nothing on its own, and expires ten minutes after the ceremony started. If the command reports that no authority was recorded, start a fresh ceremony with /fiador authority-start.

What this proves

A completed ceremony records that the attestation wallet's holder and a Discord account with authority over the application took part in one fresh ceremony — application authority, team-grade. It does not prove sole legal ownership of the application, who wrote any message, or who the human behind the wallet is. See the terms for what a Fiador result means.
