Help with a check

Fiador — human-backed attestation for Discord bots. Its separate Slack app is being prepared for an owner-only staging trial. As of 27 October 2026, no live Slack sign-in or World proof has run; general Slack enrollment is closed.

Slack owner trial

If the trial is enabled and the app says a private link expired, a check is pending, or an outcome is uncertain, stop. Do not open another World ID proof or repeat a submitted proof. A verification response can be lost after World has received a one-time proof. Only the trial operator can review the saved attempt and decide what happens next.

The owner can look for a confirmed check date in Fiador's App Home or with /fiador-whois. An installed app or a working command does not mean enrollment is enabled. The trial uses one configured workspace member; a second account is outside this test.

Public Discord attestation records

The browser verification page is not public yet. You can read the registry and founding attestation. These records contain a wallet-signed claim, but do not prove that the signer controls the named Discord application. If an independent check cannot reach the attestation host or World Chain, treat the result as unconfirmed.

Get help without sharing a proof

For a general question or reproducible public-site bug, open an issue in the Fiador repository. Describe the page or command and the general error shown. The issue is public.

Do not put a private check link, completed World proof, Slack workspace or member ID, cookie, token, wallet key, or private recovery detail in an issue. The current Slack trial is for its operator's account only; account-specific problems must stay in the operator's private review process. There is no published private support inbox for other participants, so do not invite them into this trial.
